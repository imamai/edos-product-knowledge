## The eight products

All eight are multi-tenant, hosted, reached through a browser, and priced on
the single list above.

### 1. EDOS CRM — complaint management
Complaints arrive by phone, email, web portal, WhatsApp, walk-in, social or
sales rep and converge on one register with an owner, a category, a severity
and a running clock.

Intake from four routes · named owner and status · separate acknowledgement and
resolution SLA clocks · workflows, automatic assignment and escalation ·
root-cause analysis and a corrective/preventive action register · compensation
and credit notes · contact register with full history · communication log ·
dashboards for volume, ageing, breach and backlog · satisfaction capture ·
full audit log · edos.ai assistant over your own data.

Severity is T1 critical / T2 major / T3 minor. Suits manufacturers and consumer
brands, especially food and FMCG where batch traceability matters.

### 2. EDOSPMIS — procurement
Carries a purchase from requisition to payment, with the controls enforced at
the moment somebody tries to go past them.

Requisitions against a budget line · approval chains by value and category with
delegation · budgets showing allocated, committed, pending and available ·
supplier register, RFQs, quotation comparison and award · purchase orders with
delivery dates and formal close · goods received notes with partial receipt and
inspection · three-way match with tolerances and plain-language exceptions ·
payments, AP ageing, duplicate-payment blocks · contracts with e-signature ·
procure-to-pay cycle time gate by gate · segregation of duties · full audit log.

Suits organisations buying across several departments, especially where an
audit tests approval authority and evidence of receipt before payment.

### 3. EDOS Poa — point of sale & billing
Multi-tenant POS and billing for Kenyan service businesses — photography and
media studios today, any appointment- or package-based trade tomorrow. Take
sales at a till, issue invoices, quotations and cash-sale receipts, chase
payments, track expenses, see where the money came from.

Sales terminal with cart, discounts and departments · invoices and thermal
receipts · client records with history · M-Pesa STK push · credit sales and
part payments · products, stock and barcodes · equipment inventory · suppliers
and purchase orders · expenses and profitability · analytics · staff users and
permissions · invoice delivery by email and WhatsApp · edos.ai assistant.

### 4. EDOS Hatch360 — poultry management
Poultry management and farm intelligence for Kenyan farms. *Every bird. Every
batch. Every farm.* Flocks, houses, feed, health, production and sales, with a
smallholder-farmer mode, field officers and networks.

Farm and bird limits vary by plan and are confirmed at onboarding — the plan
prices are the standard list.

### 5. EDOS LMIS — laboratory management
A clinical laboratory information system: patients, orders, specimens, tests
and results, with dedicated **microbiology, histopathology, molecular and blood
bank** workflows, quality control, equipment, departments and staff — plus its
own billing, quotations, RFQs, purchase orders, suppliers, supplier bills,
inventory and reporting.

Suits hospital and private diagnostic laboratories.

### 6. EDOS Lend — digital lending
*Digital Lending. Smarter Decisions. Better Collections.* Customer onboarding
through KYC, credit assessment, approval, disbursement, servicing, interest
accrual, repayment, arrears, collections, accounting and audit.

Suits microfinance institutions, SACCOs and digital lenders.

### 7. EDOS Master SaaS — retail & trade management
Sales, customers, inventory, procurement, quotations, suppliers and expenses,
with **eTIMS** integration for KRA electronic tax invoicing, reporting and
support — and a platform console for tenants, plans, billing and analytics.

Suits retail and trading businesses needing eTIMS compliance alongside stock
and sales.

### 8. Appointment Booking — bookings for service businesses
Multi-tenant, mobile-first appointment booking: service catalogue,
timezone-aware booking flow, guest self-service to manage, cancel or
reschedule, and a lightweight admin CMS.

Suits salons, clinics, consultants and studios.

- a bespoke system or website needs quoting — those are priced per scope

- the caller is chasing an existing invoice, payment or support ticket
- anything contractual or legal, or a commitment to a specific date
- a caller holds an older quotation at different figures — honour what they
  were given and let a human reconcile it

- invent a feature, a client name or a timeline

to you shortly." Then flag the conversation for a human.
